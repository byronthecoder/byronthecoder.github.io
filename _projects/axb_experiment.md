A web-based, browser-runnable A–X–B similarity-judgement experiment. Participants listen to three stimuli (A, X, B) and judge whether X is more similar to A or to B. The interface supports cross-speaker and cross-condition comparisons of speech samples and is suitable for phonetic convergence, voice similarity, and perceptual entrainment studies. Designed to pair with the Alternating Reading Task (ART) corpus.
